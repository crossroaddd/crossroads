Genish

About

The genesis for the Tel Aviv-based producer Itzhak Genish is a psychedelia rendered with cyclical riffs, hypnotic polyrhythms and dizzying melodies.

Genish's artistic vision shines through his releases on labels such as Multi Culti, Tau, Blue Shadow and Rebirth. His performances are marked by elaborate setups that seamlessly integrate organic and analog sounds, creating a rich tapestry of aural textures.

DJ equipment

Connectivity
