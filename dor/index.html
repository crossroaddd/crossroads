Dor Danino

About

Dor Danino is an Israeli DJ and producer whose distinctive sound blends house, psychedelic funk, and hypnotic grooves with raw dancefloor energy. Driven by a deep connection to rhythm and an instinctive understanding of the crowd, his music moves between infectious, groove-driven moments and deeper, more immersive sonic journeys.

With a growing international presence, Dor has performed at renowned venues and festivals including UNVRS Ibiza, Universo Paralello in Brazil, and The Gardens of Babylon in Amsterdam, bringing his sound to dancefloors across Europe, the Americas, and beyond. His sets are defined by an effortless flow, unexpected musical turns, and an ability to build a genuine connection with the crowd.

His productions have earned support from some of electronic music's most influential names, including Solomun, RÜFÜS DU SOL, Diplo, Keinemusik, Dixon, BLOND:ISH, and Carlita. With releases on respected labels such as Nervous Records, The Gardens of Babylon, and Maccabi House, alongside a No. 1 on Beatport's Indie Dance chart, Dor continues to establish his own identity in the global electronic music scene.

Whether behind the decks or in the studio, Dor's approach remains the same: pushing boundaries, following his instincts, and creating music that leaves a lasting impression on the dancefloor.
