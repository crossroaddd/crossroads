Mishell

Elegant sounds meet naughty feel

Old school refs
Fresh groove

Heavy producer — the majority of his sets are his own productions, which makes it unique. Expect elegant sounds with a naughty feel.

Predominantly house oriented, sharp, punchy and smooth all at once, Mishell evolves his audience with sexy, cheeky beats and a strong bass line.

His music is always fun and fresh, oscillating between darker textures and melodic build-ups with warmer tones.

Uprising artist from the Israeli scene, Mishell is currently one of the hottest names in Tel Aviv. He has been leaving his mark around the world by enticing dancefloors with his authentic groove, sharp pace and mastering the art of combining psychedelic references with house and indie dance.

Mess

Mess Makers is Mishell's core community. Supported by his local team, Mishell has built a unique brand that brings a fresh style to the local and international scene.

Mess is dedicated to producing concept-driven events, fashion, music, and more — always with a bold, sexy, naughty and uncompromising approach, fully committed to pure art. The brand allows Mishell to form a direct connection with his audience and add a new dimension to his journey.
