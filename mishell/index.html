Mishell

Old school refs
Fresh groove

MISHELL is an uprising force from the electronic scene and one of the most exciting names shaping Tel Aviv's underground right now. His sound lives where elegant meets naughty, a sharp fusion of psychedelic references, house and indie dance, driven by authentic groove and bold energy.

His music has landed on leading labels such as Hot Creations, Diynamic, Helix, Anjunadeep, Maccabi House, Catch & Release, Nu Moda and many more.

Mishell has left his mark on iconic dancefloors like Space Club, Mayan Warrior, Labyrinth UK, UNVRS Ibiza, Stereo Montreal, Bar Américas, and BOMA Brazil.

Beyond the decks, he is the founder of Mess (Mess Makers) — a creative community for concept-driven events, fashion and music. His sets are sharp, punchy and smooth, moving between dark textures and warm melodic moments, powered by sexy, cheeky rhythms and groovy basslines.

Mess

Mess Makers is Mishell's core community. Supported by his local team, Mishell has built a unique brand that brings a fresh style to the local and international scene.

Mess is dedicated to producing concept-driven events, fashion, music, and more — always with a bold, sexy, naughty and uncompromising approach, fully committed to pure art. The brand allows Mishell to form a direct connection with his audience and add a new dimension to his journey.
