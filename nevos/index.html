Nevos

Bio

nevos is a dj and producer known for his ability to truly hold a dance floor. a selector at heart and versatile on the decks, he knows exactly how to read the room — making the crowd dance from their core rather than their hands.

from intimate clubs to major festival stages, nevos delivers cheeky latin tech house grooves infused with ibiza after-party energy, creating a deep physical connection on the floor.

while still in his early steps as a producer, nevos is already receiving support from key players such as rüfüs du sol and vintage culture, with unreleased tracks being tested on some of the biggest stages.

more than just a dj, nevos is a curator of energy. nevos is the dj your dance floor needs.
