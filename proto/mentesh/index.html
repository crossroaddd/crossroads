Mentesh

The story

Years of creating and producing at the heart of the Israeli music scene have led Mentesh back to his roots in the studio.

Mentesh brings a renewed passion and a mature, sophisticated electronic sound, combining deep musicality and a unique vocal signature to create a soul-stirring journey.

Mentesh focuses on bringing raw emotion back to the dance floor and breaking the barriers between artist and audience, building a personal connection through authentic and exposed music.

Visual principles

CleanFlowDistinctElegant

The logo is built on a bold sans-serif typeface, maintaining simplicity and visual clarity. The design incorporates a subtle "flow" through a clever separation between the two parts of the word, enhancing readability and emphasizing the two syllables of the name Mentesh.

On the right, a unique MT authentication mark appears, serving as a personal signature instead of the standard trademark symbol. This choice builds credibility, sustains a high-end feel, and highlights the brand's authenticity as a distinct and recognizable hallmark.
