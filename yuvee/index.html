Yuvèe

Bio

Every great story begins with a moment of revelation. For Yuvèe, music has been an integral part of his life since childhood, but it was at the age of 10 — upon discovering EDM and deep house — that he truly found his path into the world of electronic music.

With a rich background in jazz and a degree with honors from the prestigious Academy of Music in Jerusalem, Yuvèe's sound is grounded in the timeless principles of music theory, with a commitment to blending live instruments that bring genuine depth and emotion to each track.

The contrasting energies of Jerusalem's rhythm and Tel Aviv's underground scene have shaped Yuvèe into a dynamic DJ. His sets seamlessly weave deep house and progressive house, where the sophistication of a classically trained musician meets the raw pulse of the streets.
