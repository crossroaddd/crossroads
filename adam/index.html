Adam Sellouk

About

Adam Sellouk is a standout talent in the electronic music scene, known for his captivating blend of indie dance and tech house. His impressive catalog includes releases on prestigious labels such as Experts Only, Universal, Sony, TSZR, Insomniac Records, and Hellbent showcasing his distinctive creative vision.

As a sought-after DJ, Adam has performed at premier venues and festivals worldwide, including Tomorrowland Festival (Belgium), UNVRS Ibiza, So Track Boa Festival (Brazil), Club Space (Miami), Ushuaia Ibiza, Laroc Club (Brazil), and Knockdown Center (New York).

Championed by industry heavyweights including Vintage Culture, John Summit, Adam Beyer, Adriatique, Mau P, and Tiësto, Adam’s music resonates with diverse audiences, establishing him as a respected voice in the global electronic music community.
